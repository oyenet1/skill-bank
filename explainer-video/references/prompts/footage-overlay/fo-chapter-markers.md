# Chapter Markers

## When to use
Long footage that needs navigation — a walkthrough, an interview, a lecture.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:06 | Title + what this covers | `kinetic-headline` | `whoosh` |
| 2..n | at each section | `numbered-badge` + section name | `slide` in, hold, `slide` out | `click` |
| last | final 8s | Recap `checklist` + ending | `fade-in` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{What this covers.}` / `{Section name at each boundary.}` / `{Recap.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`numbered-badge` `timeline` `slide` `checklist`

## Variants
- Markers must land on the actual section boundary in the transcript, not on
  even time divisions.
