# Analogy Bridge

## When to use
The concept is abstract. Ground it in something the learner already handles.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | The familiar thing, drawn simply | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:12–0:22 | Map: "this part = that part" | `magnifier` on both sides | `click` |
| 3 | 0:22–0:34 | Now swap in the real concept | `morph` | `whoosh` |
| 4 | 0:34–0:48 | Where the analogy breaks | `alert` marker | `alert` |
| 5 | 0:48–0:60 | Learner check | `checklist` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Familiar thing.}` / `{Mapping.}` / `{The real thing.}` / `{Where the analogy stops.}` / `{Check.}`

## Objects used
`magnifier` `morph` `split-screen` `checklist`

## Variants
- Always name where the analogy breaks. An unlabelled analogy teaches a wrong
  model.
