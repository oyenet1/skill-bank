# Collage Assembly

## When to use
Many images, one impression. The assembly is the effect.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Tiles fly in one by one | `v-click` per tile, `pop` | `pop` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:14 | Grid settles, one word lands | `highlight-box` | `whoosh` |
| 3 | 0:14–0:25 | Ending per §4 | `zoom` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{One word.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`bento-cell` `pop` `highlight-box` `particle-field`

## Variants
- Keep the grid readable: 6–9 tiles. More than that and none of them reads.
