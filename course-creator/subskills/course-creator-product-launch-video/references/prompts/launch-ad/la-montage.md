# Fast Feature Montage

## When to use
The product's breadth is the point and no single feature needs explanation.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:02 | Logo + one-word promise | `zoom` | `pop` |
| 2..n | 1.5–2s each | One real screen + one keyword each | `glitch-cut` or `whip-pan` | `whoosh` |
| last | 0:16–0:20 | CTA + destination | `highlight-box` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{One-word promise.}` / `{Feature word × n.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`whip-pan` `glitch-cut` `kinetic-headline` `browser-chrome`

## Variants
- Fast cuts need legible type. Drop a feature rather than shrink the label.
