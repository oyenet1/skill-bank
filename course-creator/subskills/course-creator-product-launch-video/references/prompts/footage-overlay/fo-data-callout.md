# Data Over Footage

## When to use
The footage is context; the numbers are the argument.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Footage establishes the scene | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:20 | Stat 1 — `counter-ticker` | `pop` | `pop` |
| 3 | 0:20–0:32 | Stat 2 — `stat-tile` | `pop` | `pop` |
| 4 | 0:32–0:40 | What the numbers mean together | `highlight-box` | `whoosh` |
| 5 | 0:40–0:45 | Ending per §4 | settle | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Scene.}` / `{Stat + unit + source.}` / `{Stat 2.}` / `{Together they mean …}`

## Objects used
`counter-ticker` `stat-tile` `highlight-box` `vignette`

## Variants
- Always source a statistic. Label `source:` on screen when the claim is load-bearing.
