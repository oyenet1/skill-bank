# Product Launch Video

Read the [screenshot-to-video workflow](references/workflow.md), [course handoff](../../references/product-launch.md), and [marketing copy](../../references/marketing-copy.md). Inspect submitted screenshots and available product or course information before writing a claim. Build a brief, script, storyboard, and per-video `style.md`; show a recognizable problem, the genuine product screen, a supported workflow, and one verified CTA. With stills alone, describe a screenshot-based demo and do not invent click results.

Read any mandatory installed video entrypoint skill and the relevant Remotion creation and rendering guidance. Animate real screens with legible pans, crops, and callouts; keep source screenshots and redacted working copies. Deliver editable Remotion source and MP4 when rendering works. TTS and CapCut handoff are separate requests; if TTS is selected, also read the [TTS subskill](../course-creator-voice-narration/SKILL.md). Store course launch outputs under `marketing/product-launch/`, register real dependencies, and inspect final frames and claims.
