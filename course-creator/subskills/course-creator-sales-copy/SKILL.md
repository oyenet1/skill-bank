# Course Marketing

Read [marketing copy](../../references/marketing-copy.md). Identify the buyer, learner, actual struggle, desired skill, available lesson or project proof, verified offer, destination, and channel. Lead with a specific question, useful promise, or short story, then give a real demonstration or sample and one clear next action. Keep the learner's level and the teacher's voice in the copy.

Use only supported prices, dates, certificates, guarantees, learner results, and testimonials. Keep promotional files under `marketing/` and register them at the course or lesson level as appropriate. Copywriting can stand alone; do not create slides, TTS, or video unless requested. For a screenshot-driven product ad, use the [product-launch subskill](../course-creator-product-launch-video/SKILL.md).
