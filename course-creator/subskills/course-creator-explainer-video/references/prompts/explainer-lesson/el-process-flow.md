# Process Flow

## When to use
The content is a procedure with a fixed order. The order is the lesson.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Why this procedure exists | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:18 | The whole `pipeline` at a glance | `fade-in` of all nodes | `pop` |
| 3..n | 10–12s each | One `step-indicator` lit at a time, detail beside it | `zoom` on the active step | `click` |
| last | 0:65–0:75 | Result + learner check | `checklist`, `success` badge | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Why.}` / `{The steps, named.}` / `{Step detail per scene.}` / `{What you get at the end.}`

## Objects used
`pipeline` `step-indicator` `node-graph` `flow-arrow` `terminal-window`

## Variants
- If the steps branch, use `node-graph` instead of `pipeline`.
