# Beat Grid

## When to use
You have a set of images and the cut is the message. Music sets the pace.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:02 | First image, full bleed | `zoom` (Ken Burns) | `whoosh` |
| 2..n | 2–3s each | Image + optional one-word label | `zoom` + `wipe` on beat | `whoosh` |
| last | final 4s | Final image + ending | `fade` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
Minimal — one word per image at most. The images carry it.

## Objects used
`zoom` `wipe` `lower-third` `grain`

## Variants
- Cut on the beat grid, not on even durations. Ask for the tempo if music is supplied.
