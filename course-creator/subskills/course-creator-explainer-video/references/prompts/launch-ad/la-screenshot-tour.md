# Screenshot Tour

## When to use
The product needs careful explanation and you have several real screens.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | What the product is, one line | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:20 | Screen 1 — full, then `zoom` to the relevant region | `zoom` | `shutter` |
| 3 | 0:20–0:32 | Screen 2 — distinct state | `wipe` | `whoosh` |
| 4 | 0:32–0:44 | Screen 3 — the result | `wipe` | `success` |
| 5 | 0:44–0:54 | What you can now do | `highlight-box` | `pop` |
| 6 | 0:54–0:60 | Ending per §4 | settle | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{What it is.}` / `{Screen n — what it shows.}` / `{What you can now do.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`browser-chrome` `phone-frame` `zoom` `wipe` `callout-box`

## Variants
- Never replace a genuine screen with a generated imitation.
