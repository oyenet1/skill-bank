# Problem Reveal

## When to use
The audience already feels the problem but has not named it. Name it, show the
cost, then turn.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | The situation, in the audience's words | `typewriter` on the hook | `typing` |
| 2 | 0:05–0:15 | The consequence, made concrete | `dim-others`, one `callout-box` | `alert` |
| 3 | 0:15–0:30 | Why the usual fix fails | `before-after` split | `whoosh` |
| 4 | 0:30–0:40 | The turn — a different approach | `morph` the frame | `pop` |
| 5 | 0:40–0:45 | Ending per §4 | settle | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Their situation, verbatim.}` / `{So this happens.}` / `{The usual fix — and why it stalls.}` / `{Instead: …}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`typewriter` `callout-box` `dim-others` `split-screen` `morph`

## Variants
- For teaching content, replace beat 3 with the misconception and beat 4 with
  the correct model (`myth-fact` pattern).
