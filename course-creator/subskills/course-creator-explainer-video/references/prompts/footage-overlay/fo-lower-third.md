# Lower Third Package

## When to use
Real footage of a person is the content. Graphics package it — the clip plays
untouched underneath.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Title + name `lower-third` | `slide` in | `whoosh` |
| 2 | 0:05–0:20 | Speaking — caption strip only | `subtitle-strip` | — |
| 3..n | as needed | Key points as `keyword-highlight` on the spoken hook | `highlight-box` | `pop` |
| mid | as needed | Data callout over the footage | `stat-tile` | `pop` |
| last | 0:82–0:90 | `lower-third` out + ending | `slide` out | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
Follow the transcript. Graphics carry names, numbers and terms — never
contradict the speech.

## Objects used
`lower-third` `subtitle-strip` `keyword-highlight` `stat-tile`

## Variants
- Caption legibility against the footage matters: use the `brand.md` caption
  style and check contrast on the actual frame, not a swatch.
