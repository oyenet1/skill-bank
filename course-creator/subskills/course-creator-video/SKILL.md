# Course Video

Read the video and CapCut sections of the [media workflow](../../references/visual-media.md) and the [video style template](../../references/video-style-template.md). Read any mandatory installed video entrypoint skill and relevant Remotion guidance before implementation. Use the teacher's chosen renderer. A Slidev deck can guide the visual sequence, but a video can also start from a brief or supplied media.

Write a concrete `style.md` for every video before scenes, including fonts, colors, tone, frame layout, presenter placement, and an effects bible. Use real course-local visuals, align scenes and captions to actual audio or footage, and synthesize narration only when requested; if TTS is requested, also read the [TTS subskill](../course-creator-tts/SKILL.md). Keep editable Remotion source, render MP4 when possible, and inspect key frames and audio. A CapCut package is separate and needs its own media clips and timecoded editing guide. Register actual inputs, outputs, and dependencies.
