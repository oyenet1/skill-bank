# Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA

## When to use
The default launch ad. One buyer, one outcome, one verified CTA.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | The problem, in the buyer's words | `typewriter` | `typing` |
| 2 | 0:04–0:09 | Product reveal — **real screen** | `zoom` + `shutter` | `whoosh` |
| 3 | 0:09–0:18 | Small real workflow | `zoom` on the action | `click` |
| 4 | 0:18–0:24 | Practical result, supported by source | `stat-tile` or `toast` | `success` |
| 5 | 0:24–0:30 | One verified CTA | `highlight-box` on the CTA | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Problem.}` / `{Product.}` / `{The one action.}` / `{Result — only if evidenced.}` / `{CTA + destination.}`

## Objects used
`browser-chrome` `zoom` `stat-tile` `toast` `highlight-box`

## Variants
- Screenshot-only: describe it as a screenshot-based demo and skip beat 3's
  implied interaction.
- Longer: expand to `la-screenshot-tour`.
