# Concept Build

## When to use
A concept the learner does not yet have. Build it in layers — one idea per
scene, each resting on the previous one.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Relatable example or story | `broll-frame`, `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:18 | What it is — plain definition | `word-by-word-reveal` | `pop` |
| 3 | 0:18–0:28 | Why it exists | `callout-box` | `click` |
| 4 | 0:28–0:42 | Where / when you use it | `flow-arrow` steps | `click` |
| 5 | 0:42–0:54 | How — the actual action or process | `command-typing` or `form-field` | `typing` |
| 6 | 0:54–0:60 | Learner check — one question | `checklist` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
Example → `What` → `Why` → `Where` → `When` → `How` → check.

## Objects used
`broll-frame` `word-by-word-reveal` `callout-box` `flow-arrow` `checklist`

## Variants
- The *How* must show an action or process. If it is abstract, redraw it as a
  `flow-arrow` or `process-flow` diagram.
