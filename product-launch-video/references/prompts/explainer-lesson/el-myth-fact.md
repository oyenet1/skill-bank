# Myth → Fact

## When to use
The learner holds a misconception. Name it before correcting it.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | The misconception, stated fairly | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:08–0:16 | Why it feels true | `callout-box` | `click` |
| 3 | 0:16–0:28 | What actually happens | `wipe` to the correct model | `alert` |
| 4 | 0:28–0:42 | Why it matters in practice | `highlight-box` | `pop` |
| 5 | 0:42–0:50 | Learner check | `checklist` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{The misconception.}` / `{Why it feels right.}` / `{What is true.}` / `{Why the difference matters.}`

## Objects used
`wipe` `callout-box` `highlight-box` `checklist`

## Variants
- Never mock the misconception. State it as a reasonable inference.
