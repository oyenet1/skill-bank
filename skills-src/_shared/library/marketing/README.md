## Sources

- **$100M Leads: How to Get Strangers to Want to Buy Your Stuff** — Alex Hormozi (2023). Hormozi's lead-generation playbook: the Lead Magnet / Core Offer pyramid, warm vs cold outreach, paid ads, and the four 'lead getters'.
- **$100M Offers: How to Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No** — Alex Hormozi (2021). The Grand Slam Offer: value equation, problem-solution stacking, scarcity/urgency/bonuses/guarantees, and pricing for premium positioning.
- **How to Sell to Nigerians** — Akin Alabi (2018). Nigeria-specific consumer psychology, trust-building, distribution, pricing and street-level selling tactics.
- **Small Business Big Money** — Akin Alabi (2017). Starting and scaling a small Nigerian business: niche selection, marketing on a tiny budget, and cash-flow discipline.
- **Sell with a Story: How to Capture Attention, Build Trust, and Close the Sale** — Paul Smith (2016). Screenwriter techniques applied to sales conversation: story structures for rapport, objection handling and closing.
- **The Psychology of Selling** — Brian Tracy (1986). Self-concept, the 80/20 rule, prospecting discipline and closing psychology. Note: this file is a condensed summary, not the full book.
