# Proof Quote

## When to use
You have a **real, attributed, permissioned** quote. Not otherwise.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:06 | The situation the quote addresses | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:06–0:22 | The quote, attributed | `word-by-word-reveal` | `whoosh` |
| 3 | 0:22–0:28 | The real screen or result referenced | `zoom` | `shutter` |
| 4 | 0:28–0:35 | Ending per §4 | settle | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Situation.}` / `{Quote — verbatim. Name, role, org.}` / `{The evidence it refers to.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`quote-card` `word-by-word-reveal` `zoom`

## Variants
- A dramatized learner story must be labelled as an example. Never present an
  invented quote as a customer's.
