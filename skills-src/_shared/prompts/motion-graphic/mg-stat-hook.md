# Stat Hook

## When to use
You have one number that carries the whole argument. The stat is the hero;
everything else is context.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:02 | Cold open: the number, oversized | `counter-ticker` counts up, `pop` on land | `pop` |
| 2 | 0:02–0:06 | What the number means, one line | `word-by-word-reveal`, hook phrase `keyword-highlight` | `whoosh` |
| 3 | 0:06–0:16 | Why it matters — 2 short lines, one `stat-tile` | `v-click` per line | `click` |
| 4 | 0:16–0:24 | The turn: what changes | `highlight-box` sweeps the hook | `whoosh` |
| 5 | 0:24–0:30 | Ending per §4 | `zoom` settle | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{NUMBER} {unit}.` / `{What it means in one line.}` / `{Why it matters.}` / `{The turn.}` / `{CTA.}`

## Objects used
`counter-ticker` `stat-tile` `keyword-highlight` `highlight-box` `gradient-mesh`

## Variants
- No source for a number? Use a `comparison-table` instead of a stat.
- Long-form: extend beat 3 to three `stat-tile`s, one per scene (concept rhythm).
