# Title Sting

## When to use
An opening or closing card: brand, title, one line. No argument, no proof.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | Logo mark resolves out of `particle-field` or `glow` | `zoom` + `fade` | `pop` |
| 2 | 0:04–0:10 | Title, one phrase | `word-by-word-reveal` | `whoosh` |
| 3 | 0:10–0:15 | Subtitle or URL, then hold | `fade-in` | `success` |

## Copy skeleton
`{Brand mark}` / `{Title, ≤6 words}` / `{One line or URL.}`

## Objects used
`glow` `particle-field` `kinetic-headline` `underline-sweep`

## Variants
- Opening sting: skip the CTA, end on the title.
- Closing sting: end on the URL and the CTA verb.
