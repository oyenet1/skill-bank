# UI Before / After

## When to use
You have genuine screenshots of both states — a redesign, a migration, a
performance improvement with visible UI.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | On screen | Motion | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | Before — real screen, named | `fade-in` | `ambient` |
| 2 | 0:10–0:18 | What was wrong, one line | `callout-box` | `alert` |
| 3 | 0:18–0:30 | After — same framing | `wipe` | `whoosh` |
| 4 | 0:30–0:36 | The measured difference | `stat-tile` | `success` |
| 5 | 0:36–0:40 | Ending per §4 | settle | `success` |
